# Controlled capabilities

A capability is controlled when it carries financial, identity, telecommunications, government, sensitive-data, safety, or other heightened obligations.

## Examples

* wallet access, payments, custody, settlement, refunds, disputes, lending, or credit;
* eSIM provisioning, activation, network services, or subscriber functions;
* high-assurance identity or government-issued attributes;
* access to authoritative government records or decisions;
* emergency, safety, surveillance, or vulnerable-person functions;
* high-risk automated decisions.

## Partner boundary

Regulated or licensed partners remain responsible for their regulated services. A technical integration does not transfer a bank's, telecommunications provider's, government body's, or other provider's license or legal authority to DigiCom.

## Separate approval

A product must identify the controlled capability, responsible provider, legal boundary, assurance level, user disclosures, data flow, incident route, evidence, operating limits, and separate authorization.

No product specification, portfolio listing, API connection, or BUILD record is itself controlled-capability approval.
