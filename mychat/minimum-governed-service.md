# Minimum governed service

The initial myCHAT service should test the smallest complete, controlled experience rather than the full product vision.

## Recommended boundary

* familiar chat;
* verified service identity;
* one defined standalone application;
* one complete service thread;
* a small set of user-initiated actions;
* status, notifications, and receipt;
* basic identity and permissions;
* application isolation;
* support and incident handling; and
* realistic device and connectivity performance.

## Initially excluded

Unless separately justified and authorized:

* live wallet operation;
* live eSIM provisioning;
* remote AI;
* MyGene action capability;
* multiple simultaneous applications;
* lending or credit;
* large-scale government integration;
* nationwide launch;
* advertising;
* open developer self-publication;
* cross-border operation; and
* high-risk automated decisions.

The first pilot application must be selected through the portfolio and BUILD process. The current specification does not select it.
