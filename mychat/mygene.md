# MyGene boundary

MyGene is the intended local-only second brain within myCHAT.

## It may

* observe permitted local myCHAT activity;
* learn local preferences and patterns;
* organize and index local information;
* retrieve locally available information;
* work quietly in the background; and
* provide summaries when the user asks.

## It must not

* send a message or email;
* submit a request, form, order, or application;
* make, approve, schedule, or cancel a payment;
* accept terms or grant consent;
* change an account, profile, permission, or setting;
* contact a person, service, application, or organization;
* operate wallet or eSIM functions;
* initiate an in-chat action; or
* perform external or irreversible activity.

## User control

Memory must remain local, inspectable, correctable, clearable, disableable, and subject to user-controlled inclusion.
