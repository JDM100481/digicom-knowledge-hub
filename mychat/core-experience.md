# Core experience

## Familiar messenger

The intended myCHAT experience includes one-to-one and group conversations, organization and service threads, message status, attachments, replies, reactions, verified service identities, notifications, search, and accessible history.

Human chat and structured service interactions should be distinguishable without feeling like separate applications.

## Actions inside chat

Authorized applications may expose actions such as viewing information, completing a form, choosing an option, uploading a requirement, confirming an instruction, submitting a request, receiving a decision, tracking status, acknowledging receipt, or contacting support.

Every action must identify the responsible provider and relevant consequence before completion.

## Wallet experience

myCHAT may expose a consistent wallet entry point through an authorized financial partner. The partner remains responsible for onboarding, custody, execution, settlement, fraud, complaints, and regulatory obligations.

## eSIM-powered connectivity

myCHAT may expose plan discovery, eSIM provisioning, activation status, top-up, renewal, and support through authorized telecommunications providers. The provider remains responsible for regulated connectivity services.

No live wallet or eSIM function is authorized by the current product specification.
