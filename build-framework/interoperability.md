# I: Interoperability

## Objective

Make the bounded, user-centered solution interoperable through explicit Protocol contracts, shared capabilities, trust boundaries, operating responsibilities, and conformance requirements.

## Required integration areas

* identity, actors, roles, permissions, consent, and delegated authority;
* sources, classification, schemas, purpose, retention, sharing, provenance, and user rights;
* service threads, actions, confirmations, states, errors, reversals, notifications, and support;
* APIs, SDKs, events, versions, environments, authentication, monitoring, security, continuity, and migration;
* accountable operator, Protocol profile, conformance scope, exceptions, partner duties, territory, and operational approval.

## myCHAT profile

Classify the product as native myCHAT, integrated, standalone conforming, transitional, or independent.

## Gate principle

Proceed only when responsibilities are explicit, contracts are versioned and testable, purposes and permissions are defined, risks have owners, claims match evidence, and a minimum governed service can be operated safely.
