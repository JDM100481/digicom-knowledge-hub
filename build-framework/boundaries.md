# B: Boundaries

## Objective

Define the real-world problem tightly enough to determine whether DigiCom should act and what remains outside the initial scope.

## Required questions

* Who experiences the problem and in what context?
* What happens today?
* What is difficult, costly, slow, unsafe, fragmented, or inaccessible?
* What evidence shows the problem is real and material?
* Which users, territory, institutions, workflows, and outcomes are included?
* What is explicitly excluded?
* Which assumptions would invalidate the initiative?

## Required outputs

A bounded problem statement, evidence summary, initial participant map, exclusions, dependencies, risks, success indicators, and a decision to proceed, narrow, research, hold, or stop.

## Gate principle

Technology, partnerships, entities, funding, and features should not expand before the problem and authority boundary are clear.
