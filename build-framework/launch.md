# L: Launch

The L in BUILD means **Launch the minimum governed service**, not launch a complete product or pursue growth before control.

## Minimum governed service

It is a real service that is:

* usable by authorized participants;
* limited by problem, users, location, duration, capability, and volume;
* supported by accountable operators;
* protected by proportionate controls;
* measured against declared criteria; and
* capable of safe pause, rollback, migration, or termination.

## Pilot charter

A launch charter defines purpose, eligible users, scope, exclusions, territory, duration, operator, partners, capabilities, data handling, security, support, measures, stop rules, and exit handling.

## Authorization boundary

The final launch decision must specify approved version, environment, participants, territory, duration, usage limits, capabilities, exclusions, budget, operator, monitoring, review schedule, and expiry.

Anything outside those limits remains unauthorized.
