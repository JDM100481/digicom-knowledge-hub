# D: Data

## Objective

Use real operational evidence to decide whether to validate, iterate, demonstrate, scale, hold, pivot, or stop.

## Development cycle

### Validate

Determine whether the problem is real, users understand and adopt the service, outcomes improve, operations function, and risk remains acceptable.

### Iterate

Change only what evidence justifies. Record the observation, evidence, proposed change, affected risks and contracts, expected result, approval, version, and post-change result.

### Demonstrate

Show repeatable user value, reliability, safety, partner performance, supportability, financial viability, Protocol behavior, and responsible ownership.

### Scale

Expand users, territory, organizations, capabilities, volume, partners, or commercial commitment only after demonstration and renewed risk review.

## Decision principle

Scale is not the default successful outcome. Evidence may correctly justify remaining small, returning to an earlier BUILD stage, holding, or stopping.
