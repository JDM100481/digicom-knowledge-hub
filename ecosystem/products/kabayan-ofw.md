# Kabayan OFW

**Status:** Controlled draft; Boundaries stage; inactive.

Kabayan OFW is a proposed cross-border application for overseas Filipino workers and their families to coordinate information, documents, household commitments, balikbayan-box shipments, support needs, and authorized services.

## Candidate modules

Family workspace, personal organizer, household support plan, remittance-reference organization, balikbayan-box coordination, documents, credentials, support referrals, and notices.

## Boundary

The application is not automatically a recruiter, employer, remittance provider, bank, telecommunications provider, logistics carrier, immigration adviser, government service, emergency service, or legal adviser. Cross-border data and regulated services require separate partner, legal, and operational authorization.
