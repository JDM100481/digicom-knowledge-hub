# BarangayOS

**Status:** Controlled draft; Boundaries stage; inactive.

BarangayOS is a proposed standalone DigiCom application for residents and authorized barangay personnel to communicate, request services, manage workflows, and maintain accountable service records through a familiar mobile-first experience.

## Candidate modules

Service information, certificate or clearance requests, incident or concern reporting, notices, resident and household context, case administration, and appropriately governed integration with other services.

## Boundary

BarangayOS must not claim official authority, issue or alter an authoritative barangay record, collect data, charge fees, or operate a pilot without explicit authorization from the responsible public body and the required operational, legal, privacy, and security controls.
