# FamilyOS

**Status:** Controlled draft; Boundaries stage; inactive.

FamilyOS is a proposed standalone application for families to coordinate responsibilities, schedules, care, household information, documents, and shared decisions through a private family workspace.

## Candidate modules

Family workspace, responsibilities and routines, calendar, care coordination, household documents, shared requests, budget coordination, family decisions, and emergency information.

## Boundary

Family participation must remain consent-based and age-appropriate. The product must not create unsupported legal authority, financial authority, surveillance, or automated decisions over children, older persons, dependents, or other vulnerable people.
