# TODA

**Status:** Controlled draft; Boundaries stage; inactive.

TODA is a proposed standalone application for recognized tricycle operators and drivers associations to coordinate membership, terminal queues, dispatch, service records, communications, and authorized passenger services.

## Candidate modules

Association membership, terminal queue and dispatch, route and terminal information, passenger access, dues and records, notices, incidents, lost items, and service readiness.

## Boundary

It does not itself grant a franchise, authorize a driver or vehicle, determine regulated fares, replace association or government decisions, operate transport, or process payments without the responsible authority and licensed providers.
