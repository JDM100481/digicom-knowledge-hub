# Kasambuhay

**Status:** Controlled draft; Boundaries stage; inactive.

Kasambuhay is a proposed standalone application for clearer, fairer, and more accountable working relationships between household workers and household employers.

## Candidate modules

Employment agreements, salary and payslip records, attendance, schedules, leave, benefits, advances and deductions, documents, concerns, and support.

## Boundary

It is not automatically a recruiter, placement agency, employer, payroll processor, financial institution, labor authority, or dispute tribunal. Payments require an authorized financial partner, and official employment or government records remain with their responsible authorities.
