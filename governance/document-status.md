## Excluded from publication

The following source categories remain outside the public GitBook:

* decision register and open matters;
* corporate and venture structures;
* developer equity, compensation, and IP negotiations;
* tax and financial governance;
* term sheets and named commercial negotiations;
* confidential assessments;
* unresolved internal decisions;
* personal data and security-sensitive implementation details.

## Effect

Publication does not change the status of a controlled source. A public summary is not approval, activation, certification, funding authority, partnership authority, or permission to operate.
