# Governance model

The locked internal DigiCom Constitution establishes the ecosystem's governing principles. This public page summarizes non-confidential operating rules and is not a substitute for the controlled document or binding legal instruments.

## Governance separation

DigiCom keeps these decisions distinct:

* portfolio inclusion;
* BUILD gate approval;
* Protocol conformance;
* technical integration validation;
* product activation;
* operational authorization;
* funding approval; and
* controlled-capability authorization.

## Steward responsibilities

The Protocol steward maintains versions, protects terminology, defines evidence requirements, reviews exceptions, records material decisions, and may restrict misleading claims or unsafe participation.

## Responsibility boundary

Banks remain responsible for banking and regulated financial services. Telecommunications providers remain responsible for network and connectivity services. Government bodies remain authoritative for their records and decisions. Application providers remain responsible for their specialized rules, workflows, support, and outcomes.

## Publication boundary

Internal decision registers, open matters, corporate and venture structures, developer and IP negotiations, tax and financial governance, confidential assessments, term sheets, security-sensitive details, and personal data are not part of the public documentation site.
