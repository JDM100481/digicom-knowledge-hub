# Governance model

DigiCom governance keeps protocol development, technical validation, and operational authorization distinct.

## Governance responsibilities

* maintain protocol definitions and version history;
* approve normative changes;
* define conformance profiles and required evidence;
* review exceptions and transitional arrangements;
* authorize controlled capabilities;
* record decisions, risks, incidents, and corrective actions;
* prevent misleading claims about integration or conformance.

## Decision records

Material architectural and governance decisions should be recorded with:

* context;
* decision;
* alternatives considered;
* consequences;
* owner;
* effective version;
* review triggers.

## Publication boundary

Public documentation explains the protocol, ecosystem, and approved developer guidance. Private commercial arrangements, personal data, security-sensitive implementation details, unresolved negotiations, and controlled internal records must remain outside the public documentation space.
